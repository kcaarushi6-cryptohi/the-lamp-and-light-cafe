Our Story

Brewed with care, shared with community

The Lamp and Light Cafe began with a simple belief: a good cup of coffee can turn a quiet hour into a lasting memory. We source our beans ethically, roast and brew them with patience, and bake our pastries fresh each morning.

Whether you're catching up with friends, working on your laptop, or enjoying a slow breakfast, our doors are open and the lamp is always lit.
